How FindIt Works

A simple, secure, 3-step university protocol designed to prevent fraudulent claims and ensure belongings reach their rightful owners.

1

Report or Search

Post a lost item report with specific locations, or browse active listings to check if a fellow student or proctor has already found it.

2

Prove Ownership

For found items, the finder sets a secret challenge question (e.g. lock-screen photo or serial digits). Only the rightful owner who answers correctly unlocks the finder's contact details.

3

Return & Earn Points

Meet safely at designated campus spots (Library reception or proctor office). Upon confirmed return, the finder is awarded 50 recognition points and climbs the campus Leaderboard.

Enterprise Campus Security

Built with Strict University Privacy & Anti-Fraud Standards

FindIt enforces cryptographic database-side hashing (Bcrypt) for secret ownership answers. Phone numbers and private identities remain completely hidden until a claim is verified by the server.

Bcrypt Ownership Hashing

Plaintext secret answers are never stored in the database or exposed via API queries.

Anti-Brute Force Lockout

Attempts are throttled after 5 failed guesses to prevent bad actors from guessing verification secrets.

One-Time Finder Recognition

Points and badges are mathematically locked to award exactly once per returned item.
